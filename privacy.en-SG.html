Privacy Policy (Singapore / Asia-Pacific)

For the Singapore / Asia-Pacific English edition: this policy explains how this website collects, uses, retains and protects visitor and enquiry data, and it also applies to all other language editions of www.maibida.com.

Data controller

Kunshan Maibida Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd. ('we') is the controller of your personal data. Address: Room 213, 2/F, Yuda Science and Technology Park, No. 6 Leshan Road, Yushan Town, Kunshan, Suzhou, Jiangsu, China. Email: mai_bida@163.com. Phone: +86 159 9567 5085.

What we collect

When you voluntarily submit the enquiry form we collect your name, email, phone, company, country/region and message, plus the submission language, source page, timestamp, consent record and policy version. We do not collect these details while you simply browse product pages.

Purposes and legal basis

The data is used only to answer your product enquiry, provide quotations and technical documentation, perform a subsequent contract, and — where you have consented — follow up on business matters. The legal bases are your explicit consent and the steps necessary to enter into or perform a contract. We do not use the data for automated decision-making or profiling.

Retention

Enquiry records are kept for 24 months from submission for follow-up and dispute handling, then deleted or anonymised. They are deleted earlier if you exercise your right to erasure.

Storage and international transfers

Records are stored on servers under our control in China with access restricted to authorised staff. To reply to enquiries from Mexico, Spain, Singapore, Vietnam, Thailand and Korea, authorised staff may access the data from outside China. We maintain the same level of protection and put confidentiality and data-processing terms in place where required.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or publish your personal data. We share it only where necessary to perform a delivery you explicitly requested, where required by applicable law, regulators or courts, or where necessary to protect our legal rights. Processors acting for us are bound by equivalent confidentiality duties.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability where applicable, and withdrawal of consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it. Email mai_bida@163.com and we will respond within the statutory period; you may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (including under GDPR/LOPDGDD, LFPDPPP, PDPA, Decree 13/2023 and PIPA as applicable).

Cookies and tracking

This site does not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Baidu Analytics or other third-party tracking scripts, and does not serve behavioural advertising. Only functionally necessary storage is used for the form and language switch. If analytics cookies are introduced, we will request your consent before enabling them.

Children

This site addresses business purchasers and industrial equipment selection. It is not directed at persons under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
