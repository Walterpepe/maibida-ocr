Instant Full Inspection with 0.2 s Response

The moment parts are ejected from the injection mold, a high-speed camera and synchronized strobe light work together to inspect all 16 mold cavities immediately, with no delay in defect detection,

and no extra waiting step is required.


360° Visual Inspection with Multi-Angle Coverage

The intelligent main unit combines multi-angle ring lighting for uniform illumination across the whole area with a high-definition high-speed camera, so even tiny flaws and short shots are captured during inspection,

delivering consistently high accuracy.


AI-Powered, Adaptive to Multiple Scenarios

In-house core AI algorithms are tailored to short-shot detection for different products, removing the need for repeated tuning and handling diverse production scenarios with ease.


Intelligent Sorting + Closed-Loop Data

Nonconforming parts are sorted automatically by a robot, with no additional equipment required and lower labor costs; inspection data connects to the enterprise MES system in real time, supporting statistical

analysis, big-data traceability, and production optimization.


 Modular Design

Distance, height, and spacing are freely adjustable for flexible setup; the unit mounts securely, fits a range of injection molding machines, and adapts to different production layouts.
