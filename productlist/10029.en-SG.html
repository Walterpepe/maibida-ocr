Product Overview

A Parallel Feeding Solution Built for Precision Manufacturing

The vision-guided parallel (Delta) feeding robot handles picking, sorting, and tray loading at high speed, with high precision and flexibility. From miniature electronic components to regular hardware parts, vision guidance enables stable loading and unloading, reducing manual handling and missed inspections.

In-House AtomCV Vision System

The standard model is equipped with a Hikvision 12 MP CCD; the in-house AtomCV_1.2 control system delivers high-precision positioning and recognition.

High-Precision Parallel Structure

Repeat positioning accuracy ±0.05 mm and placement accuracy ±0.15 mm maintain a stable cycle time even at high speed.

Multiple Models for Varied Payloads

SIF series: 0.3–1 kg payload and Ø300–Ø800 working range; SIS series: flexible combinations of pick-up units.

Flexible and Customizable

Vibratory bowls, hoppers, and special tray surfaces can be customized to suit different part geometries and line layouts.

Product Series

Two Series Covering All Loading Scenarios

The SIF series is the parallel (Delta) feeding robot itself, focused on high-speed handling and tray loading; the SIS series is a pick-up/feeding unit designed for easy line integration. The two series can be combined as needed to cover loading and unloading from micro components to medium payloads.

Application Scenarios

Suited to a Wide Range of Precision Manufacturing Industries

From micro components to uniformly shaped parts, the vision-guided parallel (Delta) feeding robot uses vision guidance for stable loading, unloading, sorting, and tray loading, helping users improve cycle times and reduce missed inspections.

Electronic Component Loading and Unloading

Vision positioning, picking, and tray loading of taped components, connectors, and small precision parts.

Hardware and Precision Part Sorting

Automatic sorting, orientation, and counting of screws, washers, and small structural parts.

Food, Household and Personal Care Packaging

High-speed sorting, tray loading, and carton packing of candies, capsules, bottle caps, and similar items.

Medical Consumable Feeding

Cleanroom feeding and tray loading of test tubes, blister packs, and medical consumables.
